# Geometry Reflex Forge — evidence-informed learning design

This rebuild treats the product as a perceptual-training environment, not a calculator.

## Learning architecture

1. Retrieval before exposure: the learner must answer before the correct mapping is shown.
2. Guidance fading: low-mastery anchors use close distractors; after two successful recalls the UI removes choices and requires free numeric recall.
3. Interleaving: two-street and three-street anchors are mixed rather than blocked.
4. Informative immediate feedback: the environment visualizes the consequence of the selected sizing by moving stack into pot across streets; wrong answers show residue/over-consumption, not merely a red X.
5. Adaptive resurfacing: missed anchors return only after intervening items; older mastered anchors regain priority across visits.
6. Transfer: realistic pot/effective-stack states require the user to derive SPR before selecting sizing.
7. Multiple representations: formula meaning, spatial runway, stack/pot simulation, anchor constellation, and numeric table context all encode the same causal structure without duplicating decorative information.
8. Segmenting and signaling: the interface exposes one primary decision loop at a time and cues the causal elements relevant to that decision.
9. Mastery progression: anchor recognition -> free recall -> spaced recall -> realistic transfer.

## Research basis used for design decisions

- Retrieval practice and test-enhanced transfer.
- Interleaved retrieval practice.
- Desirable difficulties / challenge-point framing.
- Feedback meta-analyses emphasizing informative feedback rather than reward alone.
- Simulation-based education evidence favoring interactivity, repetition, distributed practice, individualized learning, and feedback.
- Cognitive-load / multimedia-learning principles: signaling, coherence, spatial contiguity, segmentation, and useful visual representations.
- Embodied learning evidence favoring active, task-relevant interaction over passive display.

## Product rule

Animation, 3D, audio, haptics, points, and celebration may reinforce a successful retrieval, but none may disclose the answer before commitment or compete with the causal lesson. The reward must point back to *why the answer works*.
